NP Membership

Cost: Free for nonprofit organizations.

  • Listing in the chamber's member directory
  • Chamber news and community updates
  • Access to free community networking opportunities

Bronze Membership

Cost: COP 300,000 per year (sample fee).

  • All NP-level community resources
  • Business directory listing and member updates
  • Access to member networking events
  • Discounts on selected workshops and training

Silver Membership

Cost: COP 600,000 per year (sample fee).

  • All Bronze membership benefits
  • Greater discounts on events and training
  • Eligibility for home page business spotlights
  • Member promotional opportunities

Gold Membership

Cost: COP 1,200,000 per year (sample fee).

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Priority eligibility for home page spotlights
  • Featured advertising and marketing opportunities
  • Premium networking and training discounts
  • Priority invitation to chamber special events
