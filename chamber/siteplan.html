Bogotá Community Chamber

Website Planning Document | WDD 231

Connecting Businesses. Growing Together.

1. Site Name

Bogotá Community Chamber

This name represents a chamber of commerce focused on connecting businesses and supporting economic development in Bogotá, Colombia.

2. Site Purpose

The Bogotá Community Chamber website will serve as a resource hub for local businesses, entrepreneurs, residents, and investors.

The website will provide a business directory, information about networking events, membership opportunities, and resources for business growth.

Its main purpose is to support local economic development and strengthen connections within Bogotá's business community.

3. Target Market

  • Small and medium business owners in Bogotá.
  • Entrepreneurs interested in starting businesses.
  • Local residents searching for products and services.
  • Investors seeking business opportunities.
  • Professionals interested in networking events.

4. Site Goals

  • Increase visibility for local businesses through an online business directory.
  • Encourage chamber membership by explaining benefits and providing an application form.
  • Promote networking events and business workshops.
  • Support economic development in Bogotá.
  • Help residents discover local products and services.

5. User Personas

Camila - Small Business Owner

Camila is a 32-year-old entrepreneur who operates a small clothing boutique in Bogotá. She wants to advertise her business, connect with other entrepreneurs, and participate in networking events.

Andrés - Business Executive

Andrés is a 45-year-old company manager. He is interested in business partnerships, economic development opportunities, and professional networking.

Sofia - New Resident

Sofia is a 28-year-old professional who recently moved to Bogotá. She wants to discover local businesses, find services, and learn about community events.

6. User Scenarios

  • A business owner wants to join the chamber and visits the membership page to learn about benefits and application requirements.
  • A resident wants to find a local restaurant and searches the business directory.
  • An entrepreneur wants to attend a networking event and checks the homepage for upcoming events.
  • A potential investor wants to learn more about Bogotá's business environment and explores the community information.

7. SEO Plan

  • Use relevant keywords such as "Bogotá businesses", "Bogotá business directory", and "business networking in Bogotá".
  • Include unique page titles and meta descriptions.
  • Use semantic HTML and appropriate heading levels.
  • Include descriptive alternative text for images.
  • Optimize images and page loading performance.
  • Encourage backlinks from participating businesses.
